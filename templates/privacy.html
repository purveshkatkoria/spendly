Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and enabling filtering. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We use industry-standard practices to protect it from unauthorised access, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls.

You can delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all your data from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to load typefaces for the interface. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit the app. No personal or expense data is shared with Google or any other third party.

We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or any other third-party data processors.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app.
